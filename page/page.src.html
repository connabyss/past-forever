    How does it know when forever is done?
    

      

        
x = 5, then square it forever:

        

      

      
    
        
  • Settles. The gold digits never change again. After forever, Past Forever hands you the finished number and carries on.
    • 
        
  • Never settles. If the digits go round and round for good, it proves that instead.
    • 
        
  • Can't see the end. If it can't prove either one, it says so. It never guesses.
    • 
      

      
“proved” means proved by Past Forever's own rules. Loops of one name with + − × and /, towers like 3 ^ x, and x ^ x are proved a second time by a separate checker on every build.
